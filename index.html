ps1dev tools

Browser tools for PlayStation 1 development. No install, nothing uploaded anywhere.

  • timweb
    TIM converter and VRAM layout editor. Import images or existing TIMs, convert to 4/8/16bpp, lay them out in VRAM, export TIM, raw, or split pixel/CLUT.
  • loopy
    Audio loop point editor. Set and audition WAV loop points and write them into a smpl chunk, with alignment for the SPU's ADPCM blocks.
  • clutter
    Palette animation editor. Build a sequence of palettes for an indexed image and play it back, with colour cycling and HSB ramps. Export raw, CLT, or multi-CLUT TIM.
Source: github.com/ps1dev. Format specs live in standards.
